How scoring works here

Pick the question’s point value first. Correct adds it and moves to the next question. Incorrect subtracts half and keeps the question open so it can be reread for the other team; press Next question when done. Fouls are −5. Timeouts: 3 per team, plus 1 in overtime, 30 seconds each.
